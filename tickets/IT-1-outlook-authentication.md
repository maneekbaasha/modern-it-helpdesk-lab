# IT-1 — Outlook Authentication Loop

**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Category:** Incident  
**Environment:** Windows 11 / Microsoft 365 / Outlook  
**Ticketing:** Jira Service Management

---

## Scenario

You are an IT Support Technician at NovaTech.

A user from the Finance department submits the following incident through the IT Helpdesk portal.

### User request

**Summary:** Outlook keeps asking for my password

**Description:**

> Since this morning, Outlook repeatedly asks me to enter my password.
> Even after entering it, the authentication window appears again and I cannot receive new emails.
>
> My Internet connection appears to be working normally.

## Your mission

Do not immediately attempt to fix the issue.

Your objective is to troubleshoot the incident methodically.

Try to determine:

1. Is the Microsoft account itself working?
2. Is the issue affecting other devices?
3. Is the problem limited to Outlook?
4. Were there any recent changes?
5. What component could be responsible?
6. What is the least disruptive action you could take?

---

## Helpdesk rule

Before changing anything, follow:

**Symptoms → Scope → Recent changes → Hypothesis → Test → Fix → Validation**

Avoid resetting accounts, deleting data or changing configurations before you have evidence that the action is relevant.
